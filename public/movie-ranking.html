The Best Movies According to John Baran

My Top Movies of All Time

Zootopia (2016)

Spectacular animation, exciting mythology, action and adventure. A huge worldwide phenomenon.

Inside Out 2 (2024)

Funny animal characters, mystery, adventure and positive messages about friendship and teamwork. Excellent family entertainment.

Super Mario

Colourful, entertaining and educational. Helps children understand emotions, friendship and growing up.
